Meet FlowAI

Not a chatbot.
An AI that knows you.

FlowAI lives inside your workspace, so it actually knows your notes, your tasks, your schedule, and how you're doing. It's not just a chat window — it's a study partner with context.

Study Assistant

Your on-demand tutor

Ask it to explain concepts, quiz you from your own notes, generate a study plan from your calendar, or summarise a PDF you uploaded. It reads your materials so you don't have to start from scratch.

"Make flashcards from my Chapter 4 notes" "What's due this week?" "Explain this concept in simple terms" "Start a 25-min focus session on Calculus" "Summarise this PDF" "I have 3 exams next week, help me plan"
Therapist Mode

When studying feels impossible

Sometimes you don't need a to-do list. You need someone to say "hey, you're doing okay." Therapist Mode is a gentler space — no tasks, no pressure. Just a calm voice when you're overwhelmed.

"I feel overwhelmed and don't know where to start" "I've been procrastinating all week" "I'm anxious about my exam tomorrow" "I just need a break but feel guilty"
Reads your notes
FlowAI has context on everything in your workspace — notes, PDFs, tasks — so answers are specific to you, not generic.
Generates flashcards
Paste in your lecture notes or highlight a PDF paragraph and FlowAI turns it into a ready-to-review flashcard deck in seconds.
Knows your calendar
It sees your deadlines, events, and overdue items. Ask "what should I focus on today?" and get a real answer, not a template.
Helps you write
Drafting an essay outline, cleaning up messy notes, or restructuring a paragraph — FlowAI's Write mode is your editing co-pilot.
Organises for you
Ask it to sort your notes into folders, tag your tasks by subject, or create a project from scratch. It does the admin so you don't have to.
Checks in on you
FlowAI notices when you haven't studied in a few days, when a deadline is creeping up, and nudges you — gently, not annoyingly.
Study Tools

Write notes. Then actually learn from them.

Most notes get written and never looked at again. Axinote closes that loop — highlight anything, flip it into a flashcard, and your own words come back to quiz you. Spaced repetition remembers when to resurface them so you don't have to think about it.

  • Rich markdown editor — headings, lists, code blocks, the works
  • One-click convert: note → flashcard deck
  • Spaced repetition that adapts to how well you know each card
  • FlowAI can quiz you from your own notes mid-session
PDF Tools

Your textbook, but interactive

Stop switching between a PDF reader, a notes app, and a highlighter. Drop your lecture slides or textbook into Axinote, annotate right there, and ask FlowAI to explain anything you've underlined. Everything you pull from that PDF stays linked to the source.

  • Drag-in any PDF — slides, papers, past exams
  • Highlight and annotate without leaving your workspace
  • Turn highlighted passages into flashcards instantly
  • Ask FlowAI "explain this diagram" right inside the page
Stay organised

Deadlines that don't sneak up on you

Your tasks know about your calendar events. Your calendar knows about your due dates. FlowAI looks at both and tells you what actually matters today — so you're not making that decision yourself at 11pm.

  • Tasks with priority, due dates, and subject tags
  • A calendar that shows tasks and events together
  • Deadline Risk score — know before it's too late
  • "What's overdue?" — just ask FlowAI
Elite only Exam Advantage System

Your personal exam strategist.
Not just a study tracker.

Most tools tell you what to study. Exam Advantage tells you how — based on your actual sleep patterns, focus style, learning type, and stress levels. It builds a profile of how you perform, then optimises everything around it.

Study DNA Profile
Answer a short quiz once. FlowAI builds a deep profile of your learning style, chronotype, stress triggers, and focus span — then uses it to personalise everything.
Optimal session timing
Based on your natural focus window, Exam Advantage recommends when to study, how long each session should run, and how hard to push before diminishing returns kick in.
Live stress & sleep tracking
Tell FlowAI how you slept. It adjusts your plan — knowing that a 6h night means lighter recall work today, not a full study sprint.
Mock exams + weak spot analysis
Practice under real exam conditions, then get a breakdown of exactly which topics cost you points — and a targeted revision plan to close the gaps before the real thing.
